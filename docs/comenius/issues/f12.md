## Status and source

Project brief: multimodal analysis. Inspectable synchronization and quality review make this goal concrete.

## What to build

Pair two recordings in an experiment, align them using the existing synchronization capabilities, show quality and uncertain periods, and save the reviewed mapping for downstream analysis.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A researcher explicitly confirms which recordings belong together and their timing sources/units.
- [ ] Alignment displays synchronization evidence, gaps/drift, non-overlap, and uncertain or excluded intervals.
- [ ] Manual corrections and exclusions are recorded with their source inputs; changed pairings invalidate stale alignment.
- [ ] A synthetic case with known timing plus a nominated real pair meet researcher-approved tolerances, including missing-pulse and mismatch cases.
- [ ] GUI and CLI emit equivalent mapping/provenance; failed quality gates prevent falsely presenting aligned results as accepted.
